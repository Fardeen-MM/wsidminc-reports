01
Get Found — Paid Advertising
You're invisible when business owners search for environmental or zoning attorneys.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3 Research keywords: "environmental attorney Dayton" "zoning lawyer Ohio"
4 Build campaign: 3-5 ad groups by practice area
5 Write 3 ad variations per group
6 Create landing pages for each practice area
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms, add negative keywords
8 Create fresh ad copy (platforms reward newness)
9 Adjust bids based on performance data
10 Monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week
