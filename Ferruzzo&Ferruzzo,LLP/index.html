The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap

Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when families search at 9 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, forms, consultations)
3 Research keywords: "estate planning attorney Newport Beach"
4 Build 3-5 ad groups organized by intent
5 Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
6 Review search terms, add negative keywords
7 Pause underperforming ads, reallocate budget
8 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE (platforms reward fresh content)
9 Monitor competitor ads and test new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

⚠️ Reality check: Ad creative dies fast. What worked last week won't work next month. Skip a week and costs rise, performance drops. This never stops.
02

Get Answered — 24/7 Intake

The Problem: 35%+ of calls come after hours. Voicemail loses 85% of them.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1 Research answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai, LEX Reception)
2 Write intake scripts for every scenario
3 Create FAQ document for common caller questions
4 Set up call forwarding rules and lead delivery
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
5 Review call recordings for quality
6 Check for missed calls or failed transfers
7 Update scripts based on new questions
When Staff Turns Over (Every 3-6 Months)
8 Retrain new staff from scratch
9 Re-test with mock calls to verify quality
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week + retraining
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI receptionist setup & configuration
  • Custom intake scripts for estate planning
  • Qualification logic (urgency, case type, assets)
  • Calendar integration for direct booking
  • CRM integration for lead delivery
  • 30+ language support
  • <5 minute response time, 24/7/365
  • Call recording & transcription
  • Lead scoring & prioritization
  • Ongoing optimization & improvements
  • No retraining when "staff" turns over (AI doesn't quit)

You take qualified appointments. That's it.

⚠️ Reality check: Human services have turnover. Every time someone leaves, you're back to square one on training. Scripts go stale. Quality drifts.

7 Update existing pages with new information
8 Monitor keyword rankings
9 Build 1-2 backlinks through outreach
10 Request and respond to Google reviews
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Technical SEO audit & fixes
  • Page speed optimization
  • Mobile experience optimization
  • Contact form implementation
  • Trust signal placement (your 15-year Super Lawyers streak!)
  • Practice area landing pages
  • Weekly content publishing
  • Existing page updates & optimization

Your site climbs while you practice law.

04

Get Closed — CRM & Follow-Up

The Problem: Leads go cold because follow-up is manual and inconsistent.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1 Choose a CRM: Clio Grow, Lawmatics, HubSpot
2 Map your intake workflow
3 Build automated email sequences (immediate, 24hr, 72hr, 7-day)
4 Set up task reminders for phone follow-ups
Daily (Every Day, Forever)
5 Check new leads — respond within 5 minutes
6 Complete follow-up tasks
7 Update lead statuses, log all interactions
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 5-8 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • CRM setup & configuration
  • Intake workflow mapping
  • Automated email sequences
  • SMS follow-up automation
  • Task & reminder system setup
  • Lead source tracking
  • Pipeline reporting & analytics
  • Dead lead reactivation campaigns
  • Template creation & optimization
  • Ongoing sequence refinement
  • Monthly conversion analysis

Leads get nurtured automatically. You show up to consultations.

⚠️ Reality check: CRM only works if you work it. Every day. Skip a week and leads go cold. This is the most time-intensive gap to manage yourself.
You can absolutely do this yourself — many successful firms do. But be honest about the time commitment: this isn't a weekend project. It's 20-35 hours/week of ongoing work, forever.
