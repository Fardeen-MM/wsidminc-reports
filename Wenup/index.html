01

Get Found — Google Ads for Prenup Searches

The Problem: You're invisible on Google when couples actively search for prenuptial agreements.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time):
1
Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking (form fills, consultation bookings)
2
Research keywords: "prenuptial agreement UK", "prenup cost UK", "online prenup service", "fixed fee prenup"
3
Build 3-5 ad groups by intent: prenup seekers, cost comparers, postnup/cohab seekers
4
Write 3 ad variations per group — headline with "£690/person", "Both Partners Included", "5-Star Rated"
5
Create dedicated landing pages per service (not just homepage)
6
Build negative keywords list (divorce, free template, DIY prenup)
Weekly (Every Week, Forever):
7
Review search terms — add negatives for irrelevant queries
8
Pause underperforming ads, create fresh ad copy (platforms reward new creative)
9
Monitor competitor ads — what angles are Divorce-Online and Lawhive testing?
10
Check budget pacing and conversion tracking accuracy
