01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when ISOs search "MATCH list attorney" at 11 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix

  • 2 Research keywords: "MATCH list attorney," "payments lawyer," "fintech litigation"
  • 3 Build 4-6 ad groups by service (MATCH, reserves, M&A, compliance)
  • 4 Create dedicated landing pages for each practice area
  • 5 Set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager, build audience segments

WEEKLY (Forever):

  • 6 Review search terms, add negatives for irrelevant queries
  • 7 Create NEW ad creative (platforms reward fresh content)
  • 8 Monitor competitor ads—what angles are they testing?
  • 9 Adjust bids based on performance data
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER

  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting—5+ variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly—platforms reward this)
  • LinkedIn targeting: ISO owners, fintech execs, processors
