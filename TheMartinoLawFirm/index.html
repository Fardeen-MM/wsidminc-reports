01
Get Found — Paid Advertising
You're invisible when business owners search at 9 PM.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2Research keywords: "fractional general counsel Arkansas," "business attorney Little Rock"
3Build 3-5 ad groups by intent (GC, contracts, HR, compliance)
4Create dedicated landing pages (not your homepage)
Weekly (Forever)
5Review search terms, add negative keywords
6Create fresh ad copy (platforms reward new creative)
7Adjust bids, pause underperformers
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week

  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
