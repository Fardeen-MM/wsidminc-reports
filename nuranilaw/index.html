01

Get Found — Google Ads

You're invisible when the panicked parent searches "criminal lawyer Edmonton" at 2 AM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3 Research keywords: "criminal lawyer Edmonton", "DUI lawyer Edmonton", "assault charges lawyer"
4 Build campaign structure by charge type
5 Write 3 ad variations per group
6 Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms, add negative keywords
8 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE weekly (platforms reward freshness)
9 Pause underperformers, reallocate budget
10 Monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
