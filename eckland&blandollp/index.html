01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when injured mariners search at 2 AM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3 Research keywords: "Jones Act lawyer," "maritime injury attorney MA"
4 Build campaign structure: 3-5 ad groups by case type
5 Write 3 ad variations per group
6 Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms—add negatives for irrelevant queries
8 Pause underperforming ads, reallocate budget
9 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE—platforms reward fresh content
10 Monitor competitor ads and adjust positioning
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
