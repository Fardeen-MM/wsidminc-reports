01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when entrepreneurs search "E-2 visa lawyer NYC" at midnight.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2Set up conversion tracking (calls, forms, consultations)
3Research keywords: "E-2 visa lawyer NYC," "immigration attorney Spanish," etc.
4Build 3-5 ad groups by visa type (E-2, EB-5, corporate)
5Create dedicated landing pages (not your homepage)
6Set up Meta Business Manager and pixel
Weekly (Forever)
7Review search terms, add negative keywords
8CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE — platforms reward fresh content
9Pause underperformers, reallocate budget
10Monitor competitor ads for new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Bilingual landing page design
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Latin America geo-targeting strategy
  • Monthly performance reporting

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
