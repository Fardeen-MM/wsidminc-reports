01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3 Research keywords: "land use attorney NJ", "zoning lawyer", "redevelopment counsel"
4 Build campaign structure by practice area and location
5 Create LinkedIn Campaign Manager account
6 Target developers, RE investors, and municipal officials by title
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms, add negative keywords
8 Create fresh ad copy (platforms reward new content)
9 Monitor competitor ads
10 A/B test headlines and CTAs
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hrs/week FOREVER
