Get Found — Google Ads

🛠️ DIY Fix

  2. 2Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
  3. 3Research keywords: "startup lawyer NYC" + variations
  4. 4Build campaign structure: 3-5 ad groups by intent
  5. 5Write 3 ad variations per group
  6. 6Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
WEEKLY (Forever):
  1. 7Review search terms, add negatives
  2. 8Check budget pacing
  3. 9Pause underperformers, reallocate
  4. 10CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE (platforms reward fresh)
  5. 11Monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
