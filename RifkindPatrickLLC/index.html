01
Expand Reach — Meta Ads for B2B
The Problem: You're running Google Ads, but missing decision-makers on LinkedIn and Facebook.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1Set up Meta Business Manager and ad account
2Install Meta Pixel on rifkindpatrick.com for conversion tracking
3Build custom audiences: business owners, C-suite, construction industry
4Create campaign structure: awareness → consideration → conversion
5Design ad creative: images, videos, carousels for each audience
Weekly (Forever)
6Review frequency metrics—high frequency = audience fatigue
7CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE—platforms reward fresh content weekly
8A/B test headlines, CTAs, and audience segments
9Pause underperforming ads, reallocate budget to winners
⏱️ Setup: 12-18 hours
🔄 Ongoing: 4-6 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • B2B audience research and targeting strategy
  • Custom audience building (business owners, executives, industries)
  • Campaign architecture and funnel design
  • Ad copywriting—3-5 variations per audience
  • Creative design: images, videos, carousels
  • Landing page optimization for conversions
  • Pixel setup and conversion tracking
  • Fresh ad creative every week (ad fatigue is real)
  • A/B testing across all variables
  • Lookalike audience building from your best clients
  • Monthly performance reporting with insights
  • Quarterly strategy reviews and optimization
You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
02
Filter the Noise — AI Lead Qualification
The Problem: 40 emails in 90 minutes. Most aren't qualified. You're doing the sorting yourself.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1Define your ideal client profile: industry, size, case type, budget
2Create intake form with qualifying questions
3Set up email filters and rules for auto-categorization
4Build scoring system: A leads, B leads, unqualified
5Create response templates for each category
Daily (Forever)
6Review and score incoming inquiries manually
7Send appropriate template response based on score
8Update scoring criteria as you learn what converts
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hours
🔄 Ongoing: 1-2 hrs/day FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI intake system setup and configuration
  • Custom qualification logic for your practice areas
  • Intelligent scoring: case type, urgency, budget fit
  • Automatic categorization of all inquiries
  • Priority alerts for high-value opportunities
  • Auto-responses to low-fit inquiries (polite declines)
  • Warm handoff scheduling for qualified leads
  • Integration with your calendar and CRM
  • Continuous learning from your feedback
  • Monthly qualification accuracy reports
You see only pre-qualified opportunities. The noise disappears.
03
Speed to Lead — Instant Response System
The Problem: Business owners in crisis don't wait. Response time = conversion rate.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)

3Set up email/SMS alerts for new inquiries
4Configure calendar with booking link
5Train staff on response protocols
Daily (Forever)
6Monitor alerts continuously during business hours
7Respond personally within 15 minutes of alert
8Follow up on non-responses at 24hr, 72hr, 7-day intervals
⏱️ Setup: 4-6 hours
🔄 Ongoing: Constant vigilance
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI-powered instant response (<5 minutes, 24/7)
  • Intelligent conversation handling
  • Immediate qualification and routing
  • Calendar integration for direct booking
  • Personalized responses based on inquiry type
  • Seamless handoff to your team when ready
  • Multi-channel: web, email, SMS, phone
  • No training, no turnover, no sick days
  • Scales instantly during high-volume periods
  • Response time analytics and optimization
Every lead gets instant attention. You join when it matters.
04
Get Closed — CRM & Follow-Up Automation
The Problem: Leads go cold because follow-up is manual and inconsistent.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)

2Map your intake workflow: lead → qualification → consult → client
3Build automated email sequences (immediate, 24hr, 72hr, 7-day)
4Set up task reminders for phone follow-ups
5Configure lead source tracking
Daily (Forever)
6Check new leads—respond within 5 minutes during business hours
7Complete follow-up tasks assigned by system
8Update lead statuses, log all interactions
