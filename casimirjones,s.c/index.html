Get Visible — LinkedIn Thought Leadership

🛠️ DIY Fix
SETUP (One-Time):
  • 1Audit your LinkedIn profile—headline, about section, featured content
  • 2Identify 50 target connections (biotech CEOs, in-house counsel, tech transfer directors)
  • 3Create a content calendar aligned to your podcast episodes
  • 4Set up Canva templates for consistent visual branding
WEEKLY (Every Week, Forever):
  • 5Post 3-4x/week (podcast clips, patent insights, industry commentary)
  • 6Comment thoughtfully on 10-15 posts from target connections
  • 7Send 5-10 personalized connection requests to new contacts
  • 8Respond to all comments and DMs within 24 hours
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 5-8 hours/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • LinkedIn profile optimization for B2B conversion

  • Ghostwritten posts that match your voice

  • Connection request campaigns to target accounts

  • 5Build automated reminders for follow-up touchpoints
DAILY (Every Day, Forever):
  • 6Log all interactions (calls, emails, meetings) immediately
  • 7Complete daily follow-up tasks from the system
  • 8Update pipeline stages as relationships progress
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 30-60 min/day FOREVER
