01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when your ideal clients search "business development coach for lawyers."

🛠️ DIY Fix

SETUP (One-Time):

  • 1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
  • 2Research keywords: "business development coach lawyers," "lawyer coaching," "rainmaker training"
  • 3Build 3-5 ad groups by intent (coaching, training, women lawyers)
  • 4Write 3 ad variations per group with compelling headlines
  • 5Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
  • 6Set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager for professional targeting

WEEKLY (Forever):

  • 7Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 8Create fresh ad creative (platforms reward new content)
  • 9Adjust bids based on performance
  • 10A/B test headlines, images, CTAs
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week
