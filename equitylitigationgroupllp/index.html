Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

You're invisible when executives search at 10 PM. Zero ads running.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2Research keywords: "shareholder dispute attorney," "business litigation lawyer," "fiduciary breach attorney"
3Build 3-5 ad groups by practice area (equity disputes, partner buyouts, derivative actions)
4Create dedicated landing pages for each practice area
Weekly (Forever)
5Review search terms report—add negatives for irrelevant queries
6Write fresh ad creative (platforms penalize stale ads)
7Adjust bids based on time-of-day performance
8Monitor competitor ads and adjust messaging
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting—3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

04

Get Closed — CRM & Follow-Up

High-value leads go cold because follow-up is manual. Automated nurturing fixes this.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1Choose a CRM (Clio Grow, Lawmatics, or HubSpot)
2Map intake workflow: lead → qualification → consult → client
3Build email sequences (immediate, 24hr, 72hr, 7-day)
4Set up task reminders for phone follow-ups
Daily (Forever)
5Check new leads—respond within 5 minutes during business hours
6Complete follow-up tasks assigned by the system
7Update lead statuses as conversations progress
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 5-8 hrs/week
