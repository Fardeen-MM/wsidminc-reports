01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: When trustees search at 10 PM, your competitors' ads appear—you don't.

🛠️ DIY Fix

SETUP (One-Time):

1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2Research keywords: "pensions solicitor," "DB scheme advice," "bulk annuity lawyers"
3Build campaigns: 3-5 ad groups by intent (trustees, employers, advisers)
4Write 3+ ad variations per group with pensions-specific CTAs
5Create dedicated landing pages—not your homepage

WEEKLY (Forever):

6Review search terms, add negatives
7Create fresh ad copy—platforms reward new creative
8Monitor competitor ads, test new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week FOREVER
