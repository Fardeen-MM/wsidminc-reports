01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when employees search at 9 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, forms, consults)
3 Research keywords: "employment solicitor Cardiff" + variations
4 Build campaign: 3-5 ad groups by intent
5 Write 3 ad variations per group
6 Create dedicated landing pages
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms, add negatives
8 Pause underperforming ads
9 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE—platforms reward fresh content
10 Monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs
🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analysed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting—3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimisation
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimisation
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly—platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

⚠️ Reality check: Ad creative dies fast. What worked last week won't work next month. You need fresh creative EVERY WEEK to stay competitive. Skip a week and costs rise, performance drops. This never stops.

10 Re-test with mock calls
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hrs
🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week + retraining cycles
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI receptionist setup & configuration
  • Custom intake scripts for employment law
  • Qualification logic (urgency, case type, seniority)
  • Calendar integration for direct booking
  • CRM integration for lead delivery
  • 30+ language support
  • <5 minute response time, 24/7/365
  • Call recording & transcription

  • Ongoing optimisation & improvements
  • No retraining when "staff" turns over (AI doesn't quit)
