  • 2Create Meta Business Suite for Instagram/Facebook

  • 4Set modest test budget ($500-1,000/month)

WEEKLY (Forever):

  • 5Identify top-performing organic posts
  • 6Boost highest-engagement content to target audiences
  • 7Review metrics: reach, engagement, profile visits
  • 8Adjust targeting based on what converts
⏱️ Setup: 4-6 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 2-3 hrs/week
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Platform setup and pixel installation

  • Weekly content performance analysis

  • A/B testing headlines and creative
  • Retargeting campaigns for website visitors
  • Monthly ROI reporting
  • Budget optimization across platforms

Your best content reaches 10x more of the right people.

02
Repurpose Above the Haze (Video → Everywhere)

The Problem: You have a video content library that most firms would kill for. But long-form YouTube videos don't perform on LinkedIn or Instagram. Each platform needs native content.

🛠️ DIY Fix

PER VIDEO:

  • 1Watch full episode, identify 3-5 quotable moments
  • 2Clip 30-60 second segments with captions
  • 3Upload natively to LinkedIn (not YouTube link)
  • 4Create Instagram Reels from best clips
  • 5Write carousel summarizing key takeaways
  • 6Create text post with one provocative insight
  • 7Draft newsletter/email from content
⏱️ Per episode: 3-5 hrs 🔄 Weekly commitment
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Video clip extraction (3-5 per episode)
  • Professional captioning and formatting
  • Native uploads to all platforms
  • Carousel design from video content
  • Quote graphics for Instagram Stories
  • Blog post transcription and SEO
  • Email newsletter drafts
  • Cross-platform scheduling

One interview becomes 10+ pieces of content.

03
Strategic LinkedIn Growth

The Problem: 547 followers is a solid start, but pages with 1K-5K followers see 40%+ year-over-year growth. You're leaving network effects on the table.

  • 5Invite new connections to follow company page
  • 6Use polls to spark conversation

MONTHLY:

  • 7Review analytics — which posts drove followers?
  • 8Double down on winning content types
⏱️ Per attorney: 3-5 hrs/week
✨ We Handle All of This:

  • Follower growth campaigns
  • Analytics and monthly reporting
  • Competitor content monitoring

Attorneys stay visible. You stay focused on clients.
