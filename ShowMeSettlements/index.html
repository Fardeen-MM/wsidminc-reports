01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when accident victims search at 10 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3Research keywords: "car accident lawyer Kansas City"
4Build 3-5 ad groups organized by intent
5Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
6Set up Meta Business Manager + Pixel
Weekly (Forever)
7Review search terms, add negative keywords
8Pause underperforming ads, scale winners
9CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE (platforms reward fresh content)
10Monitor competitor ads weekly
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER

  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
