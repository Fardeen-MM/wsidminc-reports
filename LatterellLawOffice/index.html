Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself — no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Google Ads

You're invisible when Jacksonville families search at 9 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, forms, consults)
3 Research keywords: "estate planning attorney Jacksonville" + variations
4 Build 3-5 ad groups by intent (wills, trusts, elder law)
5 Write 3 ad variations per group
6 Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
Weekly (Forever)
7 Review search terms, add negative keywords
8 Pause underperformers, shift budget to winners
9 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE — platforms reward freshness
10 Monitor competitor ads, test new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly — platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
