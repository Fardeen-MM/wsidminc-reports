Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself — no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when attorneys search for expert witnesses.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time):
  • 1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
  • 2Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
  • 3Research keywords: "medical expert witness," "legal nurse consultant," "malpractice expert"
  • 4Build campaign structure: 3-5 ad groups by service type
  • 5Write 3 ad variations per group
  • 6Set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager for attorney targeting
Weekly (Forever):
  • 7Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 8Pause underperforming ads, reallocate budget
  • 9Create fresh ad copy (platforms reward new content)
  • 10Monitor competitor ads and positioning
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ legal terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture for B2B legal services
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page optimization for attorney conversion
  • LinkedIn targeting for law firm decision makers
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative weekly
  • A/B testing headlines and CTAs
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting

You approve the budget and take the attorney calls. That's it.
