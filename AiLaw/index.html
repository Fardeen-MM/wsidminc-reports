01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when business owners search "commercial solicitor Liverpool" at 9 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
  • 1 Create Google Ads account + conversion tracking
  • 2 Research keywords: "commercial solicitor Liverpool," "IP lawyer Merseyside," etc.
  • 3 Build campaign structure (3–5 ad groups by practice area)
  • 4 Write 3 ad variations per group + dedicated landing pages
  • 5 Set up Meta Business Manager + Pixel
  • 6 Build custom audiences targeting SME owners in the North West
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
  • 7 Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 8 Create fresh ad creative — stale ads die within 7–14 days
  • 9 Pause underperformers, reallocate budget
  • 10 Monitor competitor ads and adapt
⏱️ Setup: 15–25 hrs  |  🔄 Ongoing: 8–12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analysed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3–5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimisation
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimisation (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly — platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

Weekly (Every Week, Forever)

  • 7 Check for missed calls or failed transfers
When Staff Turns Over (Every 3–6 Months)

  • 9 Increase monitoring for 2–4 weeks until up to speed
⏱️ Setup: 8–12 hrs  |  🔄 Ongoing: 2–4 hrs/week FOREVER + retraining
