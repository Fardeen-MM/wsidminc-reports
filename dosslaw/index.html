Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when Durham Region families search at 8 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
3Research keywords: "lawyer Ajax", "attorney Durham Region"
4Build 3-5 ad groups organized by intent
5Write 3 ad variations per group
6Create dedicated landing pages
7Set up Meta Business Manager and Pixel
Weekly (Forever)
8Review search terms, add negatives
9Pause underperforming ads
10CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE—platforms reward freshness
11Monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting—3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

Weekly (Forever)
6Review call recordings for quality
7Update scripts based on new questions
8Retrain staff when turnover happens (every 3-6 months)
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week + retraining
