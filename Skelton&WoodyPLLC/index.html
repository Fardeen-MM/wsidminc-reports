The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap

Here's exactly how to build B2B visibility yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: this is ongoing work, not a one-time project.

01

LinkedIn Thought Leadership

The Problem: In-house counsel and claims managers are on LinkedIn. You're not visible to them.

🛠️ DIY Fix
  • 1 Optimize profile with "Insurance Defense | Expert Witness | Local Counsel" positioning
  • 2 Post 2-3x weekly: case law updates, coverage trends, claims handling insights
  • 3 Engage daily with posts from in-house counsel, adjusters, and industry leaders
  • 4 Publish 1-2 long-form articles monthly on Texas insurance developments
  • 5 Connect strategically: 10-15 new connections weekly to target audience
  • 6 Share wins (appropriately) and expert testimony highlights
⏱️ Setup: 3-5 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 4-6 hrs/week
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Profile optimization with B2B positioning
  • Content calendar with 2-3 posts weekly
  • Ghostwritten posts based on your expertise
  • Daily engagement with target audience
  • Long-form article creation monthly
  • Strategic connection outreach
  • Analytics and optimization
  • DM response management

You approve content and take the calls. That's it.

04

Referral Relationship CRM

The Problem: Your relationships with adjusters, in-house counsel, and referring attorneys live in your head—not in a system that nurtures them.

🛠️ DIY Fix
  • 1 Choose a CRM: Clio Grow, Lawmatics, HubSpot, or even Airtable
  • 2 Import all contacts: adjusters, in-house counsel, BigLaw partners, referring attorneys
  • 3 Tag by relationship type, last contact, matter history
  • 4 Set up quarterly touchpoint reminders
  • 5 Create email sequences for case updates, holiday greetings, industry news
  • 6 Track referral sources and ROI by relationship
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 3-5 hrs/week
