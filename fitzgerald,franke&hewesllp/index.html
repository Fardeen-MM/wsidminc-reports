The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap (Step by Step)

Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when founders search at 11 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix

SETUP (One-Time):

  • 1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
  • 2Research keywords: "startup lawyer Chicago," "tech attorney," "VC financing lawyer"
  • 3Build campaign structure: 3-5 ad groups by intent
  • 4Write 3 ad variations per group + create landing pages
  • 5Set up Meta Business Manager, install Pixel

WEEKLY (Forever):

  • 6Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 7Create NEW ad creative weekly (platforms reward fresh content)
  • 8Pause underperformers, reallocate budget to winners
  • 9Monitor competitor ads, test new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly — platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

02

Get Answered — 24/7 Intake

The Problem: 30%+ of founder inquiries come after hours. Voicemail loses 85% of them.

🛠️ DIY Fix

SETUP (One-Time):

  • 1Research answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai, LEX Reception)
  • 2Write intake scripts for every scenario
  • 3Create FAQ document for common founder questions
  • 4Set up call forwarding rules & lead delivery

ONGOING (Forever):

  • 5Review call recordings weekly for quality
  • 6Retrain staff when they turn over (every 3-6 months)
  • 7Update scripts as your practice evolves
  • 8Monitor lead delivery and follow up on issues
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week + retraining cycles
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI receptionist setup & configuration
  • Custom intake scripts for tech/startup clients
  • Qualification logic (urgency, case type, budget)
  • Calendar integration for direct booking
  • CRM integration for lead delivery
  • 30+ language support
  • <5 minute response time, 24/7/365
  • Call recording & transcription
  • Lead scoring & prioritization
  • Ongoing optimization & improvements
  • No retraining when "staff" turns over (AI doesn't quit)

You take qualified appointments. That's it.

DAILY (Forever):

  • 6Complete follow-up tasks, update statuses
