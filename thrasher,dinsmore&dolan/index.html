The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap

Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself — no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01

Get Found — Paid Advertising (Google + Meta)

You're running Meta ads but invisible on Google when business owners search at 8 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
SETUP (One-Time)
1 Create a Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2 Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills, consultations)
3 Research keywords: "business litigation attorney Chardon" + variations
4 Build campaign structure: 3-5 ad groups by practice area
5 Write 3 ad variations per group with strong CTAs
6 Create dedicated landing pages (not your homepage)
WEEKLY (Every Week, Forever)
7 Review search terms — add negatives for irrelevant queries
8 Check budget pacing and adjust bids
9 CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE — platforms reward fresh content
10 Pause underperforming ads, reallocate to winners
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER
⚠️ Ad creative dies fast. What worked last week won't work next month. Skip a week and costs rise, performance drops.
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

02

Get Answered — 24/7 Intake

35%+ of calls come after hours. Voicemail loses 85% of them.

🛠️ DIY Fix
SETUP (One-Time)
1 Research answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai, LEX Reception)
2 Request demos, compare response times
3 Write intake scripts for every practice area scenario
4 Create FAQ document for common caller questions
5 Set up call forwarding rules
WEEKLY (Every Week, Forever)
6 Review call recordings for quality
7 Check for missed calls or failed transfers
8 Update scripts based on new questions
WHEN STAFF TURNS OVER (Every 3-6 Months)
9 Retrain new staff from scratch
10 Re-test with mock calls to verify quality
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week + retraining
⚠️ Human services have turnover. Every time someone leaves, you're back to square one on training.
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • AI receptionist setup & configuration
  • Custom intake scripts for all practice areas
  • Qualification logic (urgency, case type, budget)
  • Calendar integration for direct booking
  • CRM integration for lead delivery
  • 30+ language support
  • <5 minute response time, 24/7/365
  • Call recording & transcription
  • Lead scoring & prioritization
  • Ongoing optimization & improvements
  • No retraining when "staff" turns over (AI doesn't quit)

You take qualified appointments. That's it.

Get Chosen — Website Optimization + SEO

WEEKLY (Every Week, Forever)

7 Update existing pages with new information
8 Build or earn 1-2 backlinks
9 Respond to and request Google reviews

04

Get Closed — CRM & Follow-Up

Leads go cold because follow-up is manual and inconsistent.

🛠️ DIY Fix
SETUP (One-Time)
1 Choose a CRM: Clio Grow, Lawmatics, or HubSpot
2 Map your intake workflow: lead → qualification → consult
3 Build automated email sequences
4 Set up task reminders for phone follow-ups
DAILY (Every Day, Forever)
5 Check new leads — respond within 5 minutes
6 Complete follow-up tasks
7 Update lead statuses as conversations progress
MONTHLY (Every Month, Forever)
8 Analyze conversion rates by source
9 Run "dead lead" reactivation campaigns
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 5-8 hrs/week FOREVER
⚠️ CRM only works if you work it. Every day. Skip a week and leads go cold.
