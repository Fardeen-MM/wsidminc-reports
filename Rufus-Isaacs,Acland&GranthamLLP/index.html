01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

You're invisible when artists search at 11 PM.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
  • 1 Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
  • 2 Set up conversion tracking for calls and form fills
  • 3 Research keywords: "music lawyer Los Angeles," "entertainment attorney Beverly Hills"
  • 4 Build campaign with 3-5 ad groups by intent
  • 5 Create dedicated landing pages (not your homepage)
Weekly (Forever)
  • 6 Review search terms, add negatives for irrelevant queries
  • 7 Create new ad creative—platforms reward fresh content
  • 8 Pause underperformers, reallocate budget to winners
  • 9 Monitor competitor ads for new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hours/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting—3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
