01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible in both Tampa and Asheville when people search for attorneys.

🛠️ DIY Fix

Setup (One-Time)
1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
2Set up separate campaigns for Tampa and Asheville
3Research keywords for both markets
4Build ad groups by practice area and location
5Create landing pages for each market
6Set up Meta Business Manager for FB/IG
7Install tracking pixels on your website
Weekly (Forever)
8Review search terms, add negative keywords
9CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE—platforms reward fresh content
10Adjust bids based on performance by market
11Monitor competitor ads in both markets
12A/B test headlines, images, CTAs
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 10-15 hrs/week FOREVER
