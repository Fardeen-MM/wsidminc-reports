01
Get Found — Paid Advertising (Google + LinkedIn)

The Problem: When corporate counsel, risk managers, or insurers search for defense firms in Lafayette, your firm is invisible.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking for calls and form fills
2 Research B2B keywords: "insurance defense attorney Lafayette," "commercial litigation firm Louisiana," "employment defense counsel Acadiana"
3 Build 3-5 ad groups organized by practice area (insurance, employment, maritime, commercial)
4 Write 3 ad variations per group highlighting AV Preeminent rating and 40+ years
5 Create dedicated landing pages for each practice area — NOT your general homepage
6 Set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager for B2B targeting by job title (GC, Risk Manager, Claims VP)
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
7 Review search terms report — add negatives for irrelevant consumer queries
8 Create fresh ad creative weekly — platforms reward new content, stale ads decline in 7-14 days
9 Monitor competitor ads — what angles are Davidson Meaux and Degan Blanchard testing?
10 Adjust bids by time of day and day of week based on performance data
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hrs/week FOREVER

02
Get Answered — 24/7 Intake

The Problem: 35%+ of inquiries come after hours — when corporate crises happen. Voicemail loses 85% of them.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
1 Research answering services (Ruby, Smith.ai, LEX Reception) — request demos, compare legal experience
2 Write intake scripts for every scenario: new corporate inquiry, existing client callback, urgent crisis routing
3 Create FAQ document for common caller questions (without providing legal advice)
4 Configure call forwarding, lead delivery (email, SMS, CRM), and test with mock calls
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
5 Review call recordings for quality — are they following the script correctly?
6 Update scripts for new practice areas, seasonal trends, or evolving intake criteria
When Staff Turns Over (Every 3-6 Months)
7 Retrain new staff from scratch on your scripts. Re-test with mock calls. Increase monitoring for 2-4 weeks.
⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hrs/week FOREVER + retraining cycles
