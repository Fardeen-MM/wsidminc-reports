01
Get Found — Paid Advertising (Google + Meta)
The Problem: You're invisible when founders search for "patent agent Calgary" at 10 PM.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
  • 1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
  • 2Research keywords: "patent agent Calgary", "trademark registration Alberta"
  • 3Build 3-5 ad groups by practice area
  • 4Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)
  • 5Set up Meta Business Manager, install Pixel
  • 6Build retargeting audiences for website visitors
Weekly (Forever)
  • 7Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 8Pause underperformers, reallocate budget
  • 9Create fresh ad creative (platforms reward this)
  • 10Monitor competitor ads weekly
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly — platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
