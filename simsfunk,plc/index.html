01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when in-house counsel research litigation options.

🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
  • 1 Create Google Ads account with conversion tracking
  • 2 Research keywords: "commercial litigation Nashville", "non-compete attorney TN"
  • 3 Build campaign structure: 4-5 ad groups by practice area
  • 4 Create LinkedIn Campaign Manager, install Insight Tag
  • 5 Build audience: GC titles at Nashville companies 50+ employees
Weekly (Every Week, Forever)
  • 6 Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 7 Create fresh ad copy (platforms reward new creative)
  • 8 Adjust bids based on performance by practice area
  • 9 Test new audience segments on LinkedIn
  • 10 Monitor competitor ads for new angles
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 10-15 hrs/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • LinkedIn audience building & optimization
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
