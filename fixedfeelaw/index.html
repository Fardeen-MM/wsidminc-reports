01

Get Found — Paid Advertising

The Problem: You're invisible when entrepreneurs search "business contract lawyer Texas" at night. Davis Business Law captures those clicks.

🛠️ DIY Fix

SETUP (One-Time):

  • 1Create Google Ads account at ads.google.com
  • 2Set up conversion tracking (calls, form fills)
  • 3Research keywords: "business contract lawyer Texas," "fixed fee attorney," "LLC formation lawyer Dallas"
  • 4Build 3-5 ad groups organized by service type
  • 5Write 3 ad variations per group
  • 6Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage)

WEEKLY (Forever):

  • 7Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • 8Pause underperforming ads, reallocate budget
  • 9CREATE NEW AD CREATIVE — platforms reward fresh content
  • 10Monitor competitor ads for new angles
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hours | 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hrs/week FOREVER
⚠️ Ad creative dies fast. Skip a week and costs rise.
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews

You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.
