The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap (Step by Step)

Here's exactly how to fix each gap yourself—no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01
Get Found — Paid Advertising (Google + LinkedIn)
The Problem: You're invisible when GCs and law firms search for contract talent.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time)
  • Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
  • Research keywords: "contract attorney Chicago," "legal staffing," "freelance lawyer"
  • Build LinkedIn Campaign Manager, define audience (GCs, HR directors, law firm partners)
  • Create landing pages for clients vs. talent (two audiences!)
  • Set up retargeting pixels on both platforms
Weekly (Forever)
  • Review search terms, add negative keywords
  • Refresh ad creative (platforms reward freshness)
  • Adjust bids based on day/time performance
  • A/B test headlines and CTAs
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hrs 🔄 Ongoing: 6-8 hrs/week

  • Campaign architecture for both Google + LinkedIn
  • Dual-audience strategy (clients AND talent)

  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly)
